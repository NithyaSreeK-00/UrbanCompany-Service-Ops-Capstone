# Dashboard Story — Urban Company Service Operations

---

## Story 1 — City Operations Lead

**Bengaluru has the highest SLA breach rate and should be prioritized for operational improvement.**

The dashboard shows Bengaluru completed **107 bookings** and recorded **17 SLA breaches**, resulting in an **SLA breach rate of 15.9%**. This is the highest breach rate among all six operating cities. Hyderabad follows with **14.3%**, while Delhi NCR has the lowest breach rate at **7.2%**.

The City Operations Lead should prioritize Bengaluru for SLA improvement initiatives, including workforce planning, partner scheduling, and operational monitoring. Reducing SLA breaches in Bengaluru can improve customer experience while lowering operational escalations.

---

## Story 2 — Category Lead

**Deep Home Cleaning is the strongest-performing category, while Salon for Women is comparatively under-booked.**

Deep Home Cleaning generated the highest business contribution with **176 bookings** and **₹5,08,964** in total revenue across all cities. In comparison, Salon for Women completed only **65 bookings**, the lowest booking volume among all service categories, despite generating **₹1,51,689** in revenue.

The Category Lead should continue investing in Deep Home Cleaning because it delivers both strong booking volume and revenue. For Salon for Women, the relatively low booking count suggests an opportunity to increase demand through targeted promotions, partner availability improvements, or localized marketing campaigns.
